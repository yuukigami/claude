About This Model — Key Assumptions
  • Traffic stationarity. Future daily traffic equals average daily traffic observed during the experiment. Seasonality, growth, or decline is not modeled.
  • Lift stationarity (without decay). At 0% decay the observed lift persists indefinitely. This is an optimistic upper bound.
  • No interaction effects. Does not account for interactions between this experiment and other concurrent or future experiments.
  • Revenue linearity. Each incremental conversion is worth the same dollar amount. No volume discounting or capacity constraints.
  • Decay is an approximation. Compounding daily decay is a reasonable default but is not derived from this experiment's data. Actual degradation patterns vary.
